# Comfy brandbook 2026: contextual rules

Source: [brandbook_2026_short, Figma Slides](https://www.figma.com/deck/rF8WorMHQHDZvp2dEV8AJ2). The 43 slides and their visible resource links were reviewed on 2026-10-04. Slide numbers below refer to that deck. Primary Arial Black/Montserrat and optional COMFY roles are the skill owner's explicit adaptation; the brandbook itself calls COMFY Semi-Bold its primary message font.

## Palette and typography

Slide 3: green `#2ABD13`, orange `#FC5E0F`, yellow `#FFFF00`.

Slide 34: additional pink `#FEA1D0`, purple `#64328A`, white `#FFFFFF`, light green `#85F574`, dark green `#1E9F0A`. The printed light-blue HEX `#9AE5E1` conflicts with RGB `154, 229, 255` (`#9AE5FF`). Do not choose silently or infer the official token from screenshot pixels.

Slides 18–22: COMFY Semi-Bold for main brand messages, COMFY Bold for supporting text/numbers/buttons/product details, COMFY Ultra-Condensed for numbers and short accent phrases. Uppercase is recommended for main messages; both cases are allowed for supporting text. Main-message tracking is −5% and line spacing 80%; supporting tracking follows the main face. Supporting text is no larger than the main message. In this skill, these fonts remain optional alongside the primary system defined in SKILL.md.

## Original logos (slides 5–9)

Choose the original variant for contrast: a white base on green, dark, or live photo/video backgrounds; a green base on light colored, white, or black backgrounds. Monochrome is reserved for contexts where full color cannot be used.

Preserve the original base, colors, proportions, corner radii, lettering, and arrangement. Do not distort, tilt, outline, replace lettering, recolor, or add effects. Use the supplied assets rather than rebuilding the logo as text and shapes.

## Plush texture and Пуш (slides 11–15)

Orange plush texture is a recognizability element. Use original material objects or approved static/animated Пуш resources, and preserve the green/orange balance.

Slide 11 labels two example compositions with min 30% for material/objects and min 20% for Пуш. The denominator is not stated as a universal rule; use these examples as composition guidance rather than imposing a fixed percentage on every analytical slide.

Choose an approved pose with a readable human-like silhouette. Do not lengthen limbs, deform the 3D character, or invent unofficial poses. Scale proportionally and check the visible silhouette. Keep source animation/video types when supported; select an original static asset when the delivery format needs a still image.

## Message boxes and retail prices (slides 24–28)

Apply these rules to product advertising and commercial offers:

- One box communicates one idea in a few short lines.
- White boxes describe product features; orange boxes communicate added value or benefits.
- Supporting boxes are smaller in hierarchy than the main price.
- One new price is the dominant number. The old price is smaller and struck through.
- Place the main retail price on green without a separate colored container.
- Discounts, dates, and conditions are compact secondary badges.

Do not treat business forecasts, KPI totals, or tables as retail offers. For analytical blocks, preserve their information hierarchy and assign colors based on meaning; a test's particular status/color mapping is not an official brand requirement.

## КРАЩ (slides 29–32)

Use the original КРАЩ logo/crown and the shown pairings with COMFY only when the content concerns КРАЩ. The illustrated proportional check fits three crown «Щ» widths inside the COMFY width. Preserve the original lockup and inspect the source combination before placing it; do not recreate a new pairing from typed text.

## Thematic modules (slides 33–38)

Use original shapes from the linked SVG/PDF library. Choose an arrangement suited to the message, varying module count and size while preserving proportions. Scale each module uniformly. Preserve the intended grid and connections; do not add gaps that break a joined composition. Separate independent information groups may remain separate.

Do not deform a module to fit a text box. Fit the content within a suitable original shape, choose another module, or adjust the composition. Raster conversion for a platform that cannot use SVG is acceptable when it preserves geometry and colors; narrative text stays editable.

## Равлик and seasonal assets (slides 40–42)

Равлик belongs to «Оплата частинами» and is used when there is a credit/installment offer. Do not use it as generic analytical decoration.

Use seasonal assets only in their corresponding season and context. Use the regular Пуш library for evergreen character needs. Access the original resource library rather than generating a replacement for an official character.
